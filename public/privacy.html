Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what DelhiDrive collects when you use the DelhiDrive mobile app or website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how long we keep it. It is written to meet the requirements of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Google Play's User Data policy.

In short. Renting a car to a stranger requires proving who they are, so we collect identity documents, a live photograph and, at handover, a photographic record of the vehicle's condition. We keep that record for as long as the vehicle stays in our fleet, because it is the evidence that settles a damage dispute. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

  • We do not track your location in the background, or while a rental is under way.
  • We do not read your contacts, messages, call logs or other apps.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and there are no third-party ad or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone, for any purpose.

4. How long we keep it

  • Handover photographs and video are kept for as long as the vehicle remains in our fleet, and for three years after your rental ends. A damage or deposit claim can surface long after a trip, and without the record neither of us can prove what condition the car was in.
  • Licence and Aadhaar images are kept while your account is open, so you do not have to re-submit them for every booking, and are deleted when you close it — unless a dispute or legal obligation requires us to keep them longer.
  • Booking, invoice and payment records are kept for eight years, which is the retention period required for tax and company records in India.
  • Your live booking photograph is kept for one year after the rental.

5. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may:

  • Ask what we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Correct anything wrong — a misspelt name, an old phone number.
  • Ask us to erase it, where we are not required by law to keep it. Note that we cannot delete a handover record while a related dispute or insurance claim is open.
  • Withdraw consent for anything you gave it for. Withdrawing consent for identity verification means we can no longer rent you a vehicle.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Board of India if you are unhappy with how we have handled a request.

To exercise any of these, email support@delhidrive.in. We will respond within 30 days.

Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by emailing support@delhidrive.in from the address you signed up with. We will confirm within 30 days and tell you plainly if anything must be retained for a legal reason, and for how long.

6. Keeping it safe

  • Identity documents and handover media are held in private storage, reachable only through short-lived links issued to authorised staff. They are not publicly accessible and are not indexed by search engines.
  • Database access is governed by row-level security, so one customer's account cannot read another's records even if the app were tampered with.
  • Administrative access is limited to named operations staff.
  • Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the Act requires.

7. Children

DelhiDrive is not for anyone under 21. A valid driving licence held for at least 12 months is required to book, so accounts cannot meaningfully be created by minors. If we discover we hold a child's data, we delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or who we share it with, we will update this page, change the date at the top, and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
